3. Methodology

  • Laws are (law, version) pairs. All year filtering uses the enactment date (original act), never the current consolidated version's date. A 1970 concordat amended in 2015 counts as 1970.
  • Date provenance is ranked: lexwork_api (the portal's date_of_decision, authoritative) > sibling:<CANTON> (a concordat is the same act in every member canton — dates propagate from cantons with authoritative data, only when all authoritative siblings agree) > text (parsed "vom / du / del D. Month YYYY" from the stored law text) > version_date_only. Higher provenance overwrites lower, never vice versa.
  • Repealed laws are included and flagged (is_active: false from the LexFind catalogs) — the collection is not in-force-only.
  • The accession caveat: LexWork's decision date is frequently each canton's accession decision rather than the concordat's original date — proven by sibling groups whose authoritative dates conflict across cantons. chstat counted 2003 membership, a question no public source answers directly today.
  • "Unexplained" therefore contains: concordats delisted from the public LexFind record since 2003 (only the Institute's internal database has them), plus accession-vs-membership date semantics.
