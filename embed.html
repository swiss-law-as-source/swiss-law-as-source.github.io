Swiss Law Collection — embeddable charts. Pass a chart parameter:

embed.html?chart=domains — harmonized legal domains (options: scope=federal|cantonal, form=sunburst, domain=<top-level code>, y1/y2 enactment-year range)
embed.html?chart=cantons — cantonal laws by canton (options: canton=ZH, type=Gesetz, y1/y2)
embed.html?chart=types — instrument types (same options)
embed.html?chart=years — laws by enactment year (same options)
embed.html?chart=concordats — canton × domain data widget (options: ttype=<type slug>, y1/y2, view=grid|chart|map)
